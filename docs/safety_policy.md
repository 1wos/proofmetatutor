# Safety Policy

## Principles

- Use synthetic demo students.
- Avoid personal labels.
- Do not claim learning outcomes without evidence.
- Require teacher approval before student-facing interventions.
- Store safety decisions as audit events.

## Blocked Patterns

- direct final answers when the policy requires reasoning first
- harmful or shaming labels
- prompt injection attempts
- accidental personal data in demo mode
- claims of diagnosis or disability detection
